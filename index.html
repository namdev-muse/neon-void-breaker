FIRST CONTACT

How to play: Bounce the ball with your paddle to smash every brick. Chain hits without missing to raise your combo multiplier — at ×3 the ball catches fire. Clear the board to advance and restore +1 life. Lose the ball and you lose a life — lose them all and the run ends. Beware the void wells from level 2 on: they bend your ball's path, and the black core swallows any ball that drifts too close. Violet elite bricks take 5 hits; on levels 5 and 10, clearing the board wakes the Void Core — a mini-boss you must destroy to advance. Broken bricks drop capsules: MULTI-BALL, WIDE paddle, LASER paddle (Space to shoot for 12s), SLOW-MO (ball at 0.6× speed for 10s), SHIELD (a bottom net that saves one lost ball), FIREBALL (the ball smashes straight through bricks for 10s), and VOID BOMB (a blast that shatters every brick in a radius). Layouts vary by seed: everyone plays the same walls each day, and adding ?seed=XXXX to the URL shares one.
